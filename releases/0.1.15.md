# ExQL 0.1.15

- **Drag to reorder.** Drag connection tabs to put them in the order you want, and drag saved connections to reorder them within their folder. Shift+arrow keys do the same, and both orders survive a restart. Editing a saved connection no longer sends it to the end of the list.
- **It's obvious which database you're in.** The open connection's color now fills its tab and tints the top bar, and its name sits right next to **Run SQL**. Text on the tab switches between dark and light to stay readable on any color.
- **Follow foreign keys.** In table data, foreign key cells show an arrow that opens the referenced table filtered to that row, including composite keys. **Back to …** returns you to where you were, with your filters, sort and page intact.
