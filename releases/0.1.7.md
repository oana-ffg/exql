# ExQL 0.1.7

- **Database connections** now shows saved connections and the connection form side by side. Saved connections are listed on the left, with **New connection** at the top; the new or selected connection is edited on the right. Each side scrolls on its own and the Connect and Save buttons stay visible, so you no longer scroll past the list to reach the form.
- Server and Database share a row in the connection form.
